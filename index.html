Three steps, no typing.

From a stack on the table to a valued collection.

Point the camera

Hold a comic in frame. Recognition runs continuously — you do not press a shutter or line anything up.

Pick your printing

If the issue has more than one edition, they appear side by side, with prices where available. Choose the one on your shelf.

Keep it

Add it to a collection with its grade, and to your reading queue if you like. With Premium, the total updates and the chart remembers where it started.

Questions.

Does it need a barcode?

No. Recognition works from the cover art, so books printed long before barcodes existed are identified the same way as current releases.

Why does one issue show several different prices?

Because it is several different books. A first print, a later printing and a facsimile share the same cover art and almost nothing else — mixing them into one average is the most common way a collection gets valued wrong.

Does it handle graded copies?

Yes. You can switch between a raw copy and the graded (slabbed) ladder, and the price follows the grade you pick rather than showing one figure for every condition.

Do I need an account?

Not to scan. A collection is stored so it survives reinstalling the app, but nothing stops you from opening the camera and checking a book straight away.

Can I keep track of what I read?

Yes. The Reading tab keeps an Up next queue, the days you read, your streak, star ratings and notes. Your reading log stays on your phone and is never sent to our server.

What if a comic has no price?

Not every issue has price data. You can set your own price for any comic in your collection; it is clearly marked as yours, counts in your totals and stays on your phone.

What is free and what is Premium?

Scanning, a daily allowance of scans with prices, one collection and the reading log are free. Premium adds unlimited scans with prices, unlimited collections, the total value of your collections with its chart, and export of your collections and reading log.
