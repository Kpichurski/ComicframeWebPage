Questions.

Does it need a barcode?

No. Recognition works from the cover art, so books printed long before barcodes existed are identified the same way as current releases.

Why does one issue show several different prices?

Because it is several different books. A first print, a later printing and a facsimile share the same cover art and almost nothing else — mixing them into one average is the most common way a collection gets valued wrong.

Does it handle graded copies?

Yes. You can switch between a raw copy and the CGC ladder, and the price follows the grade you pick rather than showing one figure for every condition.

Do I need an account?

Not to scan. A collection is stored so it survives reinstalling the app, but nothing stops you from opening the camera and checking a book straight away.
