The Service

ComicFrame identifies comics from their covers using your phone's camera. It shows the matching issue with its editions and variants, estimates the market value of copies in different conditions and grades where price data is available, and lets you organise the issues you own into collections whose total value the app tracks over time. You can also set your own price for a comic and keep a reading log, both stored only on your device. Scanning, prices and collections need an internet connection.

No Account Needed

You do not need to create an account. The app links your collections to a device identifier, as described in our Privacy Policy. Because there is no account, we may not be able to recover your collections or move them to another device if that identifier is lost, for example after you reset or replace your phone. You can delete your data at any time in Settings → Privacy → Delete My Data.

Free Version and Premium

ComicFrame is free to download. The free version includes a limited number of scans with prices per day (usually more on your first day), one collection and the reading log. The scan allowance resets every 24 hours. Once you reach it, you can keep scanning, but the prices of issues identified after that point stay hidden until you upgrade to Premium. We may change the free allowances.

ComicFrame Premium removes the scan limit and shows the prices hidden by the free allowance, and unlocks unlimited collections (subject to fair-use limits that protect our servers), the total value of your collections with its history chart, and export of your collections and reading log. Price data is not available for every issue, edition or condition.

Prices and Recognition

Prices shown in ComicFrame are estimates based on market data. They may be incomplete or out of date, may differ from what a dealer or buyer would pay, and are provided for information only. They are not an offer to buy or sell, not an appraisal or certification, and not financial advice. Check an issue's value independently before you buy, sell, insure or trade it.

Prices you enter yourself are your own estimates. The app marks them as your price, keeps them only on your device and includes them in the totals it shows you, but they are not market data.

Recognition can be affected by lighting, glare, sleeves, wear, and editions that look alike. Always confirm the issue and edition the app suggests before relying on it.
