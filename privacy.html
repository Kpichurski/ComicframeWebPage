Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Introduction

ComicFrame ("we", "our" or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the ComicFrame mobile application collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the ComicFrame app on iOS and Android and to this website.

The short version: ComicFrame does not ask you to create an account, does not ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number, and does not sell your data to anyone. Your comic collection lives on your device.

Information We Collect

Personal information

None. ComicFrame does not require registration and we do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, e-mail address, phone number or address. You can use every feature of the app without telling us who you are.

Camera and cover images

ComicFrame needs access to your camera in order to scan comics. The camera is only active while you are on the scanning screen. Cover images are used for one purpose — recognising which issue you are pointing at — and are not used to build a profile of you, are not used for advertising, and are not sold or shared with third parties.

You can revoke camera access at any time in your device settings. If you do, the rest of the app (your collections, search and prices) continues to work; only scanning stops.

Your collection

The issues you keep, the collections you create, your portfolio value and your scan history are stored on your device. We do not require you to upload your collection to us and we do not publish it.

Market price data

ComicFrame shows live market prices, so the app requests price information over the internet. Those requests identify the issue — for example its series, issue and printing number — not you. We do not attach your identity to them, because we do not have it.

Local storage

The app stores the following on your device:

  • Your comic collections and the issues inside them
  • Scan history and the editions you confirmed
  • Portfolio value history used to draw the value chart
  • App settings and preferences (selected game, sort order, scan mode)

How We Use Information

Information is used only to make the app work:

  • To identify the comics you scan
  • To show current market prices and price history for those issues
  • To maintain your collections and calculate what they are worth
  • To let you search, sort and browse the comic database
  • To remember your preferences between sessions
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs, using aggregate, non-identifying diagnostics

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not share it with advertisers and ComicFrame contains no advertising trackers.

We may disclose information if we are required to by law, or where it is necessary to protect our rights, property or safety, or those of our users.

Third-Party Services

ComicFrame relies on a small number of external services:

  • Comic price data providers — to supply the market prices shown in the app. They receive issue identifiers, not your identity.
  • The Apple App Store and Google Play — if you buy a subscription or make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed entirely by the store. We never see or store your payment issue details.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information the app handles, and we keep the amount of information we hold to the minimum the app needs to work. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

Your collections, scan history and preferences remain on your device until you delete them in the app, reset the app, or uninstall it. Uninstalling ComicFrame removes the data the app stored on your device.

Children's Privacy

ComicFrame is suitable for a general audience and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since the app does not collect personal information from anyone, this should not arise — but if you are a parent or guardian and you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Issue Recognition

Issue recognition matches what your camera sees against a database of comics. Recognition is not perfect: lighting, glare, sleeves, wear and lookalike reprints can all affect it, and an issue can exist in several editions at very different prices. ComicFrame shows you the editions it found and asks you to confirm the one you actually own. Always check the result before relying on a value.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data held about you — in ComicFrame's case, it is on your device, in the app
  • Delete your data — by deleting collections in the app or uninstalling it
  • Object to processing, or ask us what we hold
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Because we do not collect personal information and cannot identify you, we may be unable to link any data to you — which is, in itself, the strongest privacy protection we can offer.

App Store Compliance

ComicFrame is built to comply with Apple App Store and Google Play requirements:

  • No account required and no personal information collected
  • No advertising identifiers and no ad tracking
  • Camera permission requested only for scanning, and explained in context
  • No sale of user data

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will be highlighted in the app.
